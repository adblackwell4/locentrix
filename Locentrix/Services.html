Our Services

Locentrix delivers modern geospatial solutions designed for utilities, infrastructure, environmental projects, and organizations that rely on precise spatial intelligence.

Geospatial Consulting

High-accuracy mapping, spatial analysis, and data cleanup for utilities, municipalities, and environmental projects. We transform raw spatial data into clear, actionable insight.

Utility GIS Solutions

Asset mapping, field data workflows, network analysis, and infrastructure visualization tailored for electric, water, and gas utilities. Built for reliability and operational clarity.

Custom GIS Tools

Web-based dashboards, automation scripts, and custom geoprocessing tools built with Python, PostGIS, Leaflet, Mapbox, and modern web frameworks.

Data Management & ETL

Cloud storage, database design, ETL pipelines, and geospatial data optimization using PostGIS, Supabase, and scalable cloud infrastructure.
