About Locentrix

Locentrix is a modern GIS consulting studio built on precision, clarity, and advanced geospatial technology. We help organizations make smarter decisions through powerful spatial intelligence.

Our Mission

We believe better spatial intelligence leads to better decisions. Locentrix exists to deliver high‑accuracy mapping, advanced analysis, and custom geospatial tools that empower utilities, municipalities, and environmental organizations to operate with clarity and confidence.

Who We Are

Locentrix was founded in Lexington, South Carolina, by a GIS analyst with deep experience in the utility sector. Our work blends real‑world operational knowledge with cutting‑edge geospatial technology — creating solutions that are both technically strong and practically useful.

Our Values

Precision

Every dataset, map, and workflow is crafted with accuracy and attention to detail.

Clarity

We transform complex spatial data into clear, actionable insight.

Innovation

Modern GIS tools, automation, and cloud technology drive everything we build.

Reliability

We deliver solutions that work consistently in real‑world operational environments.

Our Story

2024 — Foundation

Locentrix begins as a specialized GIS consulting effort focused on utility mapping and analysis.

2025 — Expansion

Services expand into environmental modeling, custom GIS tools, and data automation workflows.

2026 — Modernization

Locentrix adopts a tech‑forward identity, integrating cloud GIS, Supabase, PostGIS, and modern web applications into its service offerings.
